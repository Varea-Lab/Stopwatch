# Stopwatch

**A friendly stopwatch, metronome & X-Sheet for animators**
*Created by a fellow animator*

---

Hi there! Stopwatch is a small web app built for anyone who times animation
by hand — whether you're shooting on paper, in Toon Boom, TVPaint, or straight
on a rostrum camera.

If you've ever tapped a beat, then had to convert seconds into frames on a
calculator, you know the drill. Stopwatch keeps everything in frames from the
start, so what you see on screen is what you'll actually draw on the exposure
sheet.

It's a free tool, built out of passion for the craft, and it installs like a
real app on your phone, tablet or desktop — no store, no account, works
offline once installed.

## Installation (Desktop, Android & iOS)

1. Open the app's link in your browser.

2. **On Desktop (Chrome / Edge) and Android:**
   Look for the **Install** icon in the address bar, or the **"Install app"**
   entry in the browser menu, and confirm. The app appears on your desktop /
   app drawer like any other app.

3. **On iPhone / iPad (Safari):**
   Tap the **Share** icon, then **"Add to Home Screen."**
   iOS doesn't offer an automatic install prompt like Android — this manual
   step is just how Apple does it. Once added, it opens full-screen, no
   browser bar.

4. That's it — no folders to copy, no restart needed. Once installed, it
   keeps working even with no internet connection.

## Usage & workflow tips

Stopwatch has three tools in one, switchable from the top tab bar:

### Timer
- Pick a framerate (6, 12, 24, 25, 30, 60 fps) or type a custom one
  (decimals allowed, e.g. `23.976`).
- Hit **START** and the counter runs in frames + time, frame-accurate to
  your chosen fps.
- Use **LAP** to mark an in-between while the action is playing out loud
  in your head (or out loud, no judgment).
- **RESET** clears everything and gets you back to zero.

### Metronome
- Set a beat every N frames instead of every N seconds — much more useful
  when you're thinking in exposure sheet rows rather than milliseconds.
- Great for nailing walk cycles, dialogue pacing, or any repeating action.

### X-Sheet
- Every lap you record gets logged with its frame number and timestamp.
- Hit **Export CSV** to pull the whole sheet into a spreadsheet and use it
  as a real exposure sheet reference, or to hand off timing notes to
  someone else on the crew.

## Limitations & what's next

This is a one-person, evolving project — it's a timing tool, not an
animation program: no drawing, no playback of your actual footage, no
frame-by-frame scrubbing of a video file. It just keeps the clock honest
in frames instead of seconds, so you can time by ear (or by tapping) before
you even open your animation software.

If something feels off or you'd love to see a feature added, that feedback
is genuinely welcome — this keeps getting better with real workflows in mind.
